# EngineCore Governance

## Purpose

EngineCore exists to increase justified confidence through evidence, transparent reasoning, and accountable governance.

Governance ensures that improvements to capability never compromise the integrity of the system.

---

# Governing Principles

EngineCore shall:

- Preserve justified trust.
- Preserve verified experience.
- Expose reasoning.
- Distinguish evidence from opinion.
- Distinguish confidence from certainty.
- Distinguish verification from certification.
- Remain open to qualified challenge.
- Continuously improve without compromising governing principles.

# Challenge Process

Every conclusion remains open to qualified challenge.

A challenge shall:

- Preserve the original conclusion.
- Preserve supporting evidence.
- Preserve reviewer reasoning.
- Preserve final determination.

Challenges are evidence for review.

They are not automatic proof that the original conclusion was incorrect.

---

# Learning

EngineCore learns through verified corrections.

Unverified opinion shall not become institutional knowledge.

Every validated correction should become a permanent regression test.

---

# Governance Standard

EngineCore governs how intelligence becomes justified organizational knowledge.

The governing process shall remain more stable than the technologies implementing it.
