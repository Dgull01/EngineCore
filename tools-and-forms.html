01Field documentation

Semi-Annual Inspection

Inspect kitchen fire-suppression systems with forms that adapt to the selected system and equipment. Keep hood layouts, field observations, photos and technician signatures together in one inspection.

  • Shared team access
  • System-specific forms
  • Layouts, photos & signatures
Teamwork & offline field use

Your company’s technicians can work on different inspections at the same time. If two people change the same inspection, review the saved versions before continuing; changes are not automatically combined.

Sign in and open the inspection online first. Keep entering details in that cached inspection offline, then sync when connected. Full PDF reports need a connection.

02Equipment in the field

FE Inspection

Split a shared extinguisher inspection by floor or area so technicians can work in parallel. Guided service and deficiency fields, before-and-after photos and equipment history bring the team’s findings into one PDF report.

  • Shared inspection areas
  • Guided service fields
  • Photos & PDF reports
Teamwork & offline field use

Assign areas to coordinate the team’s work. If the same extinguisher record changes on another device, review the conflicting entries before syncing.

Sign in and open the inspection online first. Work in the current cached inspection offline, then sync queued entries and photos when connected. Area assignments and full PDF reports need a connection.
