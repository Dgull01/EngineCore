The deliverable

Detailed findings, with the evidence beside them.

Findings are based on the site review, the manufacturer’s current OIM (system manual) for the installed system, and applicable local Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) guidelines and requirements. We identify the system, cite the applicable manual revision and local sources, and explain how each finding follows from the evidence.

01 · As found

Existing conditions

The installed system, protected equipment, and visible conditions recorded at the site visit, considered alongside the provider’s service report, compliance tag, deficiency list, or repair quote when available.

02 · Evidence

Why a finding was made

Site photos and references to the current OIM for the installed system and applicable local AHJ guidelines. Each conclusion connects the observed condition to the requirement being applied.

03 · Disposition

What needs attention

A plain-language explanation of documented concerns, the technical basis for required corrections, and any recommendations that go beyond those requirements. Missing information and unresolved questions are clearly identified.

Optional measured verification

Use the isometric when the pipe calculation is close.

If pipe volume may be near a manufacturer limit, or the actual routing is uncertain, exact measurements matter. A defensible calculation records the installed network piece by piece, including the fittings relevant to the controlling manufacturer method.

Existing network

Measured pipe inventory

Document each accessible run, dimension and fitting. Tags on the isometric correspond to a physical parts list and the calculation basis.

Source control

Apply the correct method

Use the identified system and current applicable manufacturer instructions. If measurements or source data remain open, the calculation remains open.

Proposed work

Repair section and parts

By separate quote, a proposed addition or modification can be drawn as its own tagged repair isometric with a parts list. It is labeled proposed so it cannot be mistaken for the existing system or completed work.
