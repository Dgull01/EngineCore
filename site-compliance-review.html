The deliverable

Detailed findings, with the evidence beside them.

The review uses a structured field record adapted from EngineCore’s semiannual documentation workflow. It records system identity, observed configuration, protected cooking equipment, and the evidence available for each determination.

01 · As found

Existing conditions

Site, system, hood, appliance, nozzle, cylinder or water-supply information, and visible field conditions recorded at the report date and time.

02 · Evidence

Why a finding was made

Photos, drawing references, and short excerpts or citations from the controlling manufacturer instructions or other applicable sources.

03 · Disposition

What needs attention

Each item is marked as supported, a documented concern, or unresolved because an essential measurement or record was unavailable. Recommended next steps stay separate from work performed.

Specific, reviewable conclusions.

We do not call a condition compliant or noncompliant from an incomplete photo or an assumed manual. Where a governing source or field measurement is missing, the report says what is needed to close the question.

The reviewer, date, time, location, source revision, and scope limitations are identified in the final record.

Optional measured verification

Use the isometric when the pipe calculation is close.

If pipe volume may be near a manufacturer limit, or the actual routing is uncertain, exact measurements matter. A defensible calculation records the installed network piece by piece, including the fittings relevant to the controlling manufacturer method.

Existing network

Measured pipe inventory

Document each accessible run, dimension and fitting. Tags on the isometric correspond to a physical parts list and the calculation basis.

Source control

Apply the correct method

Use the identified system and current applicable manufacturer instructions. If measurements or source data remain open, the calculation remains open.

Proposed work

Repair section and parts

By separate quote, a proposed addition or modification can be drawn as its own tagged repair isometric with a parts list. It is labeled proposed so it cannot be mistaken for the existing system or completed work.

Scope, stated plainly

An independent review of documented reality.

This review is not a semiannual inspection or certification.The report describes conditions observed or documented at the date and time stated on it. It does not include required inspection, testing, maintenance, or repairs. No inspection or certification tag is issued. Conclusions are limited to the areas and records made available; an authority having jurisdiction retains its approval authority.
  • The report identifies the responsible fire-protection reviewer and applicable qualifications or licenses.
  • Findings distinguish observed facts, cited requirements, proposed corrections, and unresolved questions.
  • Any maintenance or repair is separately authorized and performed under its own scope by an appropriately qualified provider.
